Is this cotton candy? Vaporwave? Trans pride? All of the above? Neither? Up to you to decide! 

Spice up your 1911 with a shiny pink and blue camo and a tiny little 'x3' face scratched into the extractor on the back of the gun. The face is drawn on to the normal map, so it reacts with lighting a movement along with the other surfaces of the gun. 

To install, hold Win+R and paste `%localappdata%\Plutonium\storage\t5\` into run and hit enter. Create a folder here entitled `images` if you do not already have one. Paste the files directly into the `images` folder. 

These textures will work in Linux as well, though the location of your `Plutonium\storage\t5\` directory will depend on your configuration.
